English line weights (src/BlurMask/Assets/AiEnglish.bin):
Darwin Bautista, baudm/parseq-small, Apache License 2.0 (LICENSE-AI-ENGINE.txt).
https://huggingface.co/baudm/parseq-small, revision a1526c3d63740e460153987f9aaf6b86aa199dc1.
Changes: FP16 storage and native C# inference. Source/model hashes: docs/ai-english-model.json.
Pretrained for Latin scene text; English cursive quality depends on writing style.

Embedded text fonts: Inter, Manrope, Source Serif 4 and STIX Two Math.
SIL Open Font License 1.1; copyright notices and complete terms are preserved in
LICENSE-FONT-Inter.txt, LICENSE-FONT-Manrope.txt, LICENSE-FONT-SourceSerif4.txt,
and LICENSE-FONT-STIXTwoMath.txt. Source revisions/hashes: docs/ai-fonts.json.
English synthetic smoke fixtures use Caveat (SIL OFL 1.1), see LICENSE-FONT-Caveat.txt.
Fonts come from https://github.com/google/fonts; no font source was modified.

PARSeq architecture/reference implementation:
Copyright 2022 Darwin Bautista. Apache License 2.0 (LICENSE-AI-ENGINE.txt).
https://github.com/baudm/parseq, revision 1902db043c029a7e03a3818c616c06600af574be.
AiHandwritingNetwork.cs adapts the reference inference to native C#, SIMD and pooled buffers.
No third-party Python engine is shipped. The remainder of the app source retains its MIT license.

Cursive smoke fixtures:
Five cropped grayscale words from image 2013.jpg, regions 0-4 in annotations_test.json,
School Notebooks RU by ai-forever (MIT), revision a10cd26104f054dc116a9dbc4a29c34b494eb9ae.
Changes: crop, grayscale, resize and normalization. The fixture decoder targets include
upstream recognition errors; docs/ai-cursive-smoke.json separately records true annotations.
